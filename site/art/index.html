Visual thinking made with AI as a collaborator — refraction, translucency and motion turned into pieces you can feel. The liquid glass + flow language grew into a way of seeing.

The GLP-1 Ripple

A molecule that edits appetite is, in economic terms, a demand shock with a prescription pad. A visual essay tracing the wave outward through the food industry, retail shelves, the job market, and health & wellness.

Enter the piece →

Liquid glass & flow is one language, not two effects. Glass gives a surface depth — refraction, translucency, a specular edge that catches light as you move. Flow gives it time — a drifting gradient field beneath, and a narrative that assembles itself as you scroll. Used together, a long argument stops being a document and becomes something you move through.
